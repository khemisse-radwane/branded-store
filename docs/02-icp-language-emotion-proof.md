# ICP, Language, Emotion, Proof

## Primary ICP

Saudi women, especially:

- Mothers managing daily meals.
- Newly married women setting up a home.
- Women who host family and guests.
- Working women who need faster meal prep.
- Women who care about kitchen organization and aesthetics.

Age range:

- Main: 24-44.
- Secondary: 45-55 for food warming and storage products.

Cities to prioritize:

- Riyadh.
- Jeddah.
- Dammam / Khobar.
- Makkah / Madinah.

## Core Pain Points

### Food Warming

- Food gets cold before everyone sits.
- Guests arrive late.
- Multiple dishes lose heat.
- Reheating ruins the meal or creates extra work.
- During Ramadan and gatherings, timing matters.

### Rice Storage

- Rice bags look messy.
- Rice spills in the pantry.
- Moisture and insects are a fear.
- Measuring rice is annoying.
- Kitchen organization matters when guests or family see the space.

### Vegetable Prep

- Chopping takes too long.
- Onion smell and tears.
- Messy cutting boards.
- Fear of cuts.
- Need to prepare salad, potatoes, carrots, onions, and garlic quickly.

## Language Style

Use Saudi-friendly Modern Arabic. Keep it natural and simple.

Good:

- "سفرتك"
- "عزائمك"
- "مطبخك"
- "بدون فوضى"
- "يوفر عليك وقت"
- "جاهز للضيوف"
- "دفع عند الاستلام"

Avoid:

- Overly formal Arabic.
- Aggressive hype.
- Fake medical/scientific wording.
- Generic machine-translated product copy.

## Proof Framework

Each product page needs proof from four angles:

1. **Visual proof**
   - Demo images.
   - Before/after.
   - Close-ups of material and parts.

2. **Social proof**
   - Star rating block.
   - Saudi-style names or initials.
   - City labels.
   - UGC/review cards.

3. **Authority proof**
   - Material details.
   - Safety features.
   - Cleaning instructions.
   - Product dimensions.

4. **Operational proof**
   - COD.
   - WhatsApp confirmation.
   - Exchange for damaged/defective item.
   - Clear delivery timeline.

## Suggested Review Copy

Use seeded placeholders only if clearly marked in code as mock data until real reviews exist. Do not mislead in production.

Examples:

- **أم نورة - الرياض**: "صارت السفرة تبقى دافئة وقت العشاء، خصوصا إذا تأخر أحد."
- **سارة - جدة**: "حافظة الأرز رتبت الدولاب ووقفت الفوضى."
- **ريم - الدمام**: "القطاعة وفرت علي وقت السلطة والتحضير اليومي."

## Science and Practical Authority

Use practical explanation, not fake science.

Food warming:

- Consistent warmth helps food stay pleasant for longer.
- Timer and auto shut-off increase convenience and safety.
- Silicone-style surface is easier to wipe and store if product supports it.

Rice storage:

- Airtight storage helps reduce exposure to moisture and dust.
- Clear viewing window helps track quantity.
- Measuring cup reduces waste and mess.

Vegetable chopper:

- Container catches cut vegetables directly.
- Interchangeable blades reduce switching between multiple tools.
- Hand guard and cleaning brush support safer, cleaner use.
