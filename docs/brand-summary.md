# Dafa Kitchen Brand Summary

## Brand Name

Arabic: **مطبخ دفا**

English: **Dafa Kitchen**

## Identity

مطبخ دفا is a trusted kitchen tools curator for Saudi homes. The brand does not sell random viral gadgets. It selects practical tools that solve visible daily kitchen problems: faster prep, cleaner storage, and a more organized sink/counter area.

The identity should feel:

- Warm and Saudi-home familiar
- Practical, not flashy
- Organized and confident
- Premium but not cold
- Trustworthy for COD customers

## Brand Positioning

**مطبخ دفا هو بيت اختيار أدوات المطبخ العملية للبيت السعودي.**

We help women make the kitchen calmer, cleaner, and easier without renovating, replacing appliances, or buying complicated tools.

Every product should pass the Dafa standard:

- Solves a real daily kitchen pain
- Easy to understand in seconds
- Useful after the impulse fades
- Clear enough to confirm by phone
- Practical enough to receive and keep
- Looks organized and premium in the kitchen

## ICP Language

Speak to Saudi women with respectful, warm, practical language:

- “تعرفين اللحظة...”
- “بعد الغسيل”
- “وقت الغداء”
- “الدولاب يصير أرتب”
- “السطح يرجع فاضي”
- “تخلصين أسرع وتنظفين أقل”

Avoid exaggerated miracle claims. Authority comes from clarity, product selection standards, COD trust, phone confirmation, visible use cases, and the 30-day golden guarantee.

## Color Direction

Primary color: **Deep Kitchen Olive — `#4F5F46`**

Why: olive feels calm, domestic, premium, and kitchen-relevant. It gives more authority than bright colors while still feeling warm for a Saudi home brand.

Supporting colors:

- Warm Ivory: `#FAF7EF`
- Brass Gold: `#C8943D`
- Date Brown: `#4A3329`
- Soft Sage: `#A7B79A`

The store should feel like a curated kitchen house: warm ivory backgrounds, deep olive authority sections, brass CTAs/details, and restrained brown text.
