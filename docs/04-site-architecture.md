# Site Architecture

## Required Folders

The coder must create:

```text
frontend/
backend/
docs/
docker-compose.yml
README.md
```

## Frontend Routes

Use Next.js App Router.

Required routes:

- `/` home page.
- `/products` collection page.
- `/products/dafaya-food-warmer` product page for دفاية الطعام الكهربائية.
- `/products/rice-dispenser` product page for حافظة الأرز الذكية مع كوب قياس.
- `/products/vegetable-cutter-set` product page for مجموعة تقطيع الخضار الاحترافية.
- `/about` about us page.
- `/contact` contact page.
- `/thank-you` thank-you page.
- `/privacy` privacy policy.
- `/returns` returns/exchange policy.
- `/delivery` delivery policy.

## Global Layout

The site is Arabic-first and RTL.

Header:

- Right side: brand logo icon in circle or soft rectangle.
- Next to icon: Arabic brand name **مطبخ دفا**.
- Below or near it: English **Dafa Kitchen** in smaller text.
- Navigation menu.
- Cart button.

Menu items:

- الرئيسية
- المنتجات
- من نحن
- تواصل معنا

Footer:

- Brand block.
- Products links.
- Policies.
- Contact/WhatsApp.
- COD and support notes.
- Domain text: `dafakitchen.shop`.

## Home Page Structure

1. Top announcement bar:
   - **دفع عند الاستلام داخل السعودية - تأكيد الطلب عبر واتساب**

2. Header.

3. Hero section:
   - Right: Arabic copy and CTA.
   - Left: image composition showing the three products or kitchen scene placeholder.
   - Headline: **مطبخ أدفى، أسرع، وأنظف كل يوم**
   - Subheadline: **اختيارات عملية للمطبخ السعودي تساعدك في حفظ حرارة الأكل، ترتيب الأرز، وتحضير المكونات بسرعة.**
   - CTA: **تسوقي الآن**

4. Trust strip:
   - دفع عند الاستلام
   - تأكيد عبر واتساب
   - دعم عربي
   - استبدال عند وجود عيب

5. Problem pillars:
   - الطعام يبرد بسرعة.
   - أكياس الأرز تسبب فوضى.
   - التقطيع يأخذ وقت.

6. Product collection preview:
   - 3 product cards.

7. Brand authority section:
   - "لماذا مطبخ دفا؟"
   - Explain curated Saudi kitchen essentials.

8. Social proof section:
   - Review cards.
   - City labels.

9. How ordering works:
   - اختاري المنتج.
   - اطلبي بالدفع عند الاستلام.
   - نؤكد طلبك عبر واتساب.
   - يصلك الطلب.

10. Final CTA band.

## Product Page Structure

Each product page must include:

1. Product hero:
   - Text right, images left on desktop.
   - Sticky offer selector on desktop if practical.
   - Offer choices and CTA.

2. Pain section:
   - Clear emotional problem.

3. Demo / benefits section:
   - Alternating text/image layout.

4. What is included.

5. Why it works:
   - Practical explanation.
   - Material/safety notes.

6. Social proof.

7. Offer selector repeated.

8. FAQ.

9. Cross-sell section:
   - "كملي تجهيز مطبخك".

10. Sticky mobile CTA.

## Collection Page

Purpose:

- Make the brand look like a curated kitchen collection.
- Show all three products with consistent cards and trust.

Sections:

- Collection hero.
- Filter/sort optional but not necessary for 3 products.
- Product grid.
- Bundle explanation.
- Trust / COD block.

## Contact Page

Must include:

- WhatsApp CTA.
- Contact form placeholder.
- Working hours.
- Order confirmation explanation.
- FAQ links.

## About Page

Tell the story:

- Built for Saudi homes.
- Practical tools for daily cooking, storage, and family meals.
- Quality-selected products.
- COD and Arabic support.

Do not claim manufacturing ownership. Say "نختار" and "نوفر" rather than "نصنع" unless manufacturing becomes true.
